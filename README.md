### HerSaathi

# AI-Powered Women's Rights, Safety & Support Navigator

> Helping women understand their rights, navigate challenges, and discover reliable support — with accessible, responsible AI.
---

## 🌍 About the Project

HerSaathi is an AI-assisted platform designed to make information about women's rights, safety, digital protection, and support services easier to understand and access.

Women may face challenges in identifying the right resources, understanding legal rights, navigating government services, or finding reliable support organisations. Relevant information is often scattered across multiple websites and may be difficult to understand.

HerSaathi aims to bridge this information gap through a user-friendly platform that helps users identify their concerns, explore relevant information, understand available options, and navigate verified support resources.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Women often struggle to access reliable, understandable, and actionable information regarding their rights, safety, legal remedies, and support services.

Information is fragmented across government portals, legal resources, helplines, NGOs, and online platforms. This creates barriers to awareness, accessibility, and informed decision-making.

HerSaathi aims to make this information more accessible through a structured, AI-assisted navigation experience.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

HerSaathi will provide a guided platform where users can:

- Identify and categorise their concerns.
- Understand relevant rights and available options in simple language.
- Discover support organisations and services.
- Access personal and digital safety information.
- Better understand digital consent, privacy notices, and app permissions.

The platform is designed as an information and navigation tool—not a replacement for professional legal advice or emergency services.

---

## 🧩 Core Modules

### 1. 🔎 Identify My Problem
A guided interface that helps users identify and categorise their concerns, making it easier to navigate to relevant information and resources.

### 2. ⚖️ Know My Rights
Accessible, plain-language information about women's rights, relevant laws, legal protections, and possible options, supported by reliable sources.

### 3. 🤝 Find My Support
A searchable directory of verified support resources, including relevant organisations, services, and official assistance channels.

### 4. 🛡️ Protect Myself

Safety information and digital protection resources, including:

- Personal safety awareness
- Cybercrime and online harassment awareness
- Digital identity protection
- Online fraud awareness
- Reporting and support pathways

#### DigitalRaksha
A digital safety component focused on helping users recognise online risks and understand protective steps and reporting options.

#### ConsentClarity
A digital consent awareness component designed to explain privacy notices, app permissions, terms and conditions, and digital consent in simple language.

## 🔐 Responsible AI & Safety

HerSaathi is being designed with the following principles:

- **Privacy by Design:** Minimise the collection of personal and sensitive information.
- **Responsible AI:** AI-generated explanations should not be treated as definitive legal advice.
- **Source Transparency:** Legal and support information should be linked to reliable sources wherever possible.
- **User Safety:** Avoid unsafe recommendations and provide appropriate support pathways.
- **Accessibility:** Use simple language and a user-friendly design.
- **Human Support:** Encourage appropriate professional or official assistance when required.
